Background & motion

The background also respects your operating system's reduced-motion preference. Turning it on here is deliberate — it is not silently forced by the system.

Out of scope (honest limits)

No real authentication, email, push notifications, payments, PDF/Excel export, or external database. Roles are views with logic-level permissions in this browser only. Do not use role views as a security boundary for sensitive data.
